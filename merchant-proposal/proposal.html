You stay in control

  • You decide quantity, price and pickup time
  • Same-day items only, packed to your usual hygiene standard
  • Bag value is always clearly higher than its price
  • Your regular menu prices are never shown or discounted
  • Allergen and “contents vary” notice shown to customers
  • Pause or leave any time, no penalty

Food safety, liability and permit responsibilities will be set out in a short pilot agreement. Please review it against your own local permit requirements before signing.

Common questions

Will it cheapen my brand? Customers see a “surprise bag”, not your price tags. Your menu prices stay exactly as they are.

Won’t my regulars just switch to the cheap bag? They might. That’s why we track it. If regulars are replacing full-price purchases, we adjust bag count or stop. Your decision.

What if no one buys? You pay nothing. You can also post fewer bags.

What if the bag isn’t good enough? We set a minimum value rule with you. Bad bags kill repeat buyers, so it’s in both our interests.

How much of my time does this take? About 5 minutes at closing, plus a 30-minute setup call.
